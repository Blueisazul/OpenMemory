## 1. Executive Summary & Current State Audit

Sub-phase F5.2 successfully established the foundational persistence engine in `src/storage.ts`:
* **Data Models**: `ResearchRecord`, `KnowledgeItem`, `ProvenanceMetadata`, `ResearchFilter`.
* **Safeguards**: 10 KB per research payload ceiling, max 10 items limit, `sanitizeSecrets` regex filter, atomic `.json` persistence in `.openmemory/knowledge/researches/`.
* **API Surface**: `saveResearch`, `getResearch`, `listResearches`, `queryKnowledgeItems`, `deleteResearch`.

### The Core Problem Solved by F5.2.1
While OpenMemory can now store and query research items, **OpenMemory must NOT attempt to perform research itself** (crawling, searching, repository inspection) nor rely on a noisy passive log of all tool executions.

OpenCode already possesses:
* Web search & fetching (`WebSearch`, `WebFetch`).
* Codebase exploration & search (`grep_search`, `view_file`, `list_dir`).
* Subagent research runtimes (`Scout`, `Explore`).
* SOP & Skill guidance (`AGENTS.md`, Skills engine).
* Plugin lifecycle hooks & MCP protocol host.

This audit establishes the **most reliable, native, zero-dependency mechanism** for capturing high-signal synthesized knowledge from OpenCode without placing an unsustainable cognitive burden on the agent or introducing system noise.

## 3. Subagent Workflow Analysis (`Scout` & `Explore`)

Subagents such as `Scout` and `Explore` execute as isolated, transient sessions in OpenCode to perform fast code exploration and web research.

### Critical Subagent Constraints & Findings
1. **Transient Context**: Subagent context windows are discarded upon task completion.
2. **Direct Persistence Risk**: Allowing subagents to invoke `openmemory_record_knowledge` directly leads to fragmented, incomplete, or duplicate research records.
3. **Primary Agent Responsibility**: The **Primary OpenCode Agent** receives the subagent's summary output and is uniquely qualified to evaluate whether the findings impact the overall project plan, architecture, or handoff state.

### Recommended Subagent Flow

```text
               PRIMARY OPENCODE AGENT
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
       │ Invocations                         │
       ▼                                     ▼
 [Scout Subagent]                     [Explore Subagent]
 (Code & Dependency Research)         (Architecture Exploration)
       │                                     │
       └──────────────────┬──────────────────┘
                          │
                          ▼ Summaries returned
               PRIMARY OPENCODE AGENT
                          │
             (Synthesizes & Validates)
                          │
                Is Knowledge Reusable?
             ┌────────────┴────────────┐
             │ YES                     │ NO
             ▼                         ▼
   openmemory_record_knowledge    Discard Transient
             (MCP)                  Subagent Logs
             │
             ▼
      ResearchRecord
      KnowledgeItem
      ProvenanceMetadata
```

## 4. Automatic Capturing vs Explicit Synthesis

To maintain a high **Signal-to-Noise Ratio**, OpenMemory enforces a strict separation between automatic observation and explicit synthesis:

```text
               OPENCODE TOOL EXECUTION
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
       │                                     │
 [tool.execute.after Hook]            [Agent Response Synthesis]
 (Passive Observability)              (Cognitive Evaluation)
       │                                     │
       ▼                                     ▼
 Extract Provenance Metadata          Synthesize Knowledge Item
 - URLs fetched                       - Key Finding / Conclusion
 - Repositories accessed              - Epistemological Class (FACT/ADR)
 - Tool names & timestamps            - Provenance linkage
 - Scrub Secrets                      - Noise Policy (<10 KB, <10 items)
       │                                     │
       ▼                                     ▼
 Log Session Provenance               openmemory_record_knowledge
 (.openmemory/logs/events.jsonl)      (.openmemory/knowledge/researches/)
```

### What OpenMemory Captures Automatically (Plugin)
* Tool names, timestamps, and call IDs (`WebSearch`, `WebFetch`, `grep_search`).
* Extracted URLs, domain names, and repository names.
* Active session ID and compaction context injections.
* **Sanitized Secrets** (API keys, Bearer tokens).

### What MUST Be Recorded Explicitly (Agent via MCP)
* Synthesized technical findings, architectural decisions, and constraints.
* Epistemological classifications (`FACT`, `OBSERVATION`, `FINDING`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `CONCLUSION`).
* Categorized items (`SOURCE`, `REPOSITORY`, `FINDING`).
* Summary narrative and relation to ADRs (`relatedAdrId`).

## 5. ResearchRecord to ADR Relationship Mapping

Not all research leads to an Architectural Decision Record (ADR), but significant research often informs one.

```text
   ResearchRecord (RES-001)
     │
     ├── KnowledgeItem 1 (SOURCE: OpenCode SDK Definition)
     ├── KnowledgeItem 2 (FINDING: Plugin hooks support progressive transform)
     └── relatedAdrId ──► ADRRecord (ADR-005: Hybrid Knowledge Capture Architecture)
```

* **Storage Rule**: `ResearchRecord` includes an optional field `relatedAdrId?: string`.
* **Flow**: When a research session leads to an accepted decision, the agent creates the ADR via `openmemory_save_adr` and links `relatedAdrId` in `openmemory_record_knowledge`.

## 6. Project Instruction Design (`AGENTS.md`)

We verified that incorporating an explicit project instruction block into `AGENTS.md` is the most direct, native way to guide agent behavior in OpenCode.

### Recommended `AGENTS.md` Guideline Section (Contract Lock)

```markdown
## OpenMemory Knowledge Capture SOP
When performing research, code exploration, or dependency audits whose findings have future project value:
1. Conduct research using OpenCode native capabilities (Scout, Explore, WebSearch, WebFetch).
2. Synthesize findings and filter out raw tool logs, verbose diffs, or unparsed HTML.
3. If the knowledge is reusable for future sessions or decisions, record it via MCP tool `openmemory_record_knowledge`.
4. Categorize items cleanly (SOURCE, REPOSITORY, FINDING) and classify epistemologically (FACT, HYPOTHESIS, CONCLUSION).
5. If the research results in an architectural decision, link it to an ADR using `relatedAdrId`.
```

## 7. Open Source Audit (Build vs Integrate Reassessment)

We audited existing open-source agent memory projects to determine if an existing framework should be integrated:

* **MemGPT / Letta**: Requires dedicated Python backend servers, PostgreSQL/pgvector databases, and external LLM API endpoints. Violates zero-dependency and local-first constraints.
* **Zep / CozoDB**: Requires Docker containers and external service orchestrations. Violates zero-dependency requirement.
* **LangChain / LlamaIndex Memory Modules**: Python-heavy, non-native to OpenCode TypeScript plugin runtime.

### Audit Verdict
No external Open Source dependency is required or suitable. OpenMemory's native TypeScript architecture (`src/storage.ts` + `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk`) provides the optimal local-first, zero-dependency solution.

## 8. Answers to 11 Mandatory Success Criteria Questions

1. **¿Cómo sabe OpenCode que una investigación debe conservarse?**  
   A través de las directrices del proyecto en `AGENTS.md` y la SOP Skill `skill-research-synthesis`, que definen los criterios de relevancia cuando una investigación aporta valor futuro al proyecto.
2. **¿Quién decide qué conocimiento es relevante?**  
   El **Agente Principal de OpenCode**, el cual evalúa los resultados de las herramientas y subagentes en función del objetivo activo de la sesión.
3. **¿Cómo se registra?**  
   Invocando la herramienta MCP `openmemory_record_knowledge` con un payload validado en formato JSON-Schema.
4. **¿Cómo participan Scout y Explore?**  
   `Scout` y `Explore` realizan la exploración rápida en sesiones transitorias y devuelven sus resúmenes al Agente Principal, el cual sintetiza y persiste el conocimiento relevante en OpenMemory.
5. **¿Qué captura automáticamente OpenMemory?**  
   Metadatos de procedencia pasiva (URLs, repositorios, herramientas invocadas, timestamps) mediante hooks del plugin, eliminando previamente secretos e información sensible.
6. **¿Qué debe registrar explícitamente el agente?**  
   El conocimiento sintetizado (`ResearchRecord` e `KnowledgeItem`s con clasificaciones epistemológicas y resúmenes estructurados).
7. **¿Cómo se evita almacenar ruido?**  
   Mediante las restricciones de la **Noise Policy** (límite de 10 KB por investigación, máximo 10 ítems, sanitización `sanitizeSecrets`, anotación `DATA ONLY`) y rechazando el guardado automático de outputs brutos.
8. **¿Cómo se recuperará posteriormente?**  
   A través de la herramienta MCP `openmemory_query_knowledge`, la CLI `openmemory query`, e inyección de contexto mediante `experimental.session.compacting`.
9. **¿Podemos hacerlo con mecanismos nativos de OpenCode?**  
   **SÍ.** Utilizando exclusivamente `AGENTS.md`, Skills nativas de OpenCode, Plugin Hooks de OpenCode y el SDK oficial de MCP.
10. **¿Existe una alternativa Open Source madura que debamos integrar en lugar de desarrollar?**  
    **NO.** Ninguna alternativa OSS cumple con los principios de zero-dependency, local-first y ejecución dentro del runtime de plugins de OpenCode.
11. **¿Qué debe contener exactamente F5.3?**  
    F5.3 debe extender `src/mcp.ts` registrando `openmemory_record_knowledge` y `openmemory_query_knowledge`, con validación Zod y conexión directa a los métodos `saveResearch` y `listResearches`/`queryKnowledgeItems` de `StorageEngine`.
